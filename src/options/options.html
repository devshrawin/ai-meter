Headroom settings

How Headroom works · what it reads, what the real / estimate / banner tags mean, and how to add a site.

General

The bar fills as you use your limit and shows the details when you point at it or focus it. Iris only appears in the pill style. Reload open tabs after changing it if the bar does not appear.

Iris

Iris is the cat who lives on the meter. Her mood follows your usage; click her and she reacts.

Other AI sites

Add any AI chat site that isn't built in. Chrome asks for access to that one site only. Headroom then counts your sends there (estimate), and watches for the site's own limit notices and rate-limit errors.

    Estimate rules

    Where a site doesn't publish usage numbers, Headroom counts your sends locally and compares against these limits. Sites with real numbers only use these as a fallback. Rules are matched top to bottom against the model name; first match wins. Limit 0 means "just count". Set these to match your plan.

    Back up your settings

    Save your estimate limits, thresholds and Iris options to a file, or load them back (on a new computer, or after reinstalling). A backup holds settings only: no usage numbers, no message counts, no debug log. Sites you added need Chrome's permission again, so use Add site for those after importing.

    Debug

    Stores the last 60 matching requests/responses locally. Request bodies (your prompts) are never stored.
