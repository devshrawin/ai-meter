Welcome to Headroom

See how much of your AI usage limit is left, right on the page, before you hit the wall.

Getting started takes no setup

  1. Open an AI site you already use: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Kimi, DeepSeek, Qwen, Le Chat, Copilot, Meta AI or Poe.
  2. A small pill appears in the corner of the page with your percentage used and when it resets. Click it for the details, drag it to move it, or focus it with the keyboard and press Enter.
  3. Click the Headroom icon in the toolbar to see every service in one place.

Sign in to the site as you normally do. Headroom never asks for a password and never signs in for you.

Read the tags: real, estimate, banner

Not every site shows usage, so Headroom tells you where each number comes from.

✓ real
A solid chip with a tick. The number comes from the provider itself, the same figure the site's own usage page shows. Headroom reads real numbers for Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity and Kimi (and ChatGPT's Codex usage) wherever the site exposes them.
≈ estimate
A dashed chip with "≈". The site shows no usage, so Headroom counts the messages you send and compares them with a limit. These limits are published figures that change often, so set them to match your plan in Settings.
⚑ banner
A chip with a flag. The site itself told you that you hit a limit (a "limit reached" notice or a rate-limit error), so Headroom shows it as full until the time the site gives.

Iris, and the colours

The shape beside the percentage tells you the level, so it reads even without colour: a filled dot (green) under 75%, a half dot (amber) from 75%, a triangle (red) from 90%, a square (dark red) at 100%, and a dashed ring when there is no number. On the bars, small gaps mark 75% and 90%. Iris, the cat on the pill, shows the same thing: calm while you have plenty left, worried near the limit, stressed near the end, and asleep when you have run out. She is optional: switch her off in Settings and the meter stays.

Prefer something quieter? In Settings, Meter style can show a thin bar along the top or bottom of the page instead of the pill. Point at it to see the details.

What it reads, and what it never does

  • It reads your usage numbers from the AI sites you use, from your own logged-in tab, and notes that you sent a message and which model, to count them.
  • It never stores the text of your messages, has no server and no account, and sends nothing anywhere except to the site you are already on.
  • Everything is kept on your computer. Read the privacy policy.

Another AI site?

Open Settings, type the site's address under Other AI sites, and add it. Chrome asks for access to that one site only. Headroom then counts your sends there and watches for the site's own limit notices.
